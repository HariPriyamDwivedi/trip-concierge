# Lisbon

Lisbon rewards slow walkers. The city spreads across steep hills above the Tagus
river, so expect cobbled climbs and sudden viewpoints — the locals call them
miradouros — around almost every corner. The best months are April, May and
September, when the light is soft and the summer crowds have thinned.

Getting around is easy without a car. The metro covers the centre and the
airport, trams climb the hills, and the historic Tram 28 doubles as a sightseeing
route through Alfama and Graça. Buy a rechargeable Viva Viagem card and tap it on
trams, buses and the metro alike.

For food, skip the tourist strips and eat where the tiles are older than the menu.
Grilled sardines and bacalhau are the staples, and a pastel de nata straight from
the oven in Belém is worth the queue. Evenings belong to fado, the mournful local
song, best heard in a small Alfama tavern rather than a dinner-show hall.
