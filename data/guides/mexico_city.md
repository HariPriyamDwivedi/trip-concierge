# Mexico City

Mexico City is vast, high and surprisingly green, sitting at 2,240 metres so the
altitude can leave you short of breath for a day or two — take the first morning
slowly. The dry season from November to April is the easiest time to visit, with
warm days and cool, clear evenings.

Getting around is cheapest by metro, which is extensive and runs on flat-fare
tickets, though it gets packed at rush hour. For short hops, app-based rides are
affordable and spare you the crush; cycling is pleasant on Sundays when a long
stretch of Paseo de la Reforma closes to cars.

The city is a feast. Street tacos al pastor carved from a spit, tamales from a
morning stall, and mole in Coyoacán are all essential. Beyond food, the
Teotihuacan pyramids are a day trip north, the Frida Kahlo Museum anchors leafy
Coyoacán, and Chapultepec park holds a castle and world-class museums.
